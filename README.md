# Kessler Auto Works

> Practice project. Kessler Auto Works is a fictional business, and every name, address, phone number, email and review below is made up. Feel free to use this project for your own practice site.

## Client brief

### Overview

Kessler Auto Works is a fictional, family-owned auto repair shop in Durham, NC, made up for a practice client project.

- **Owner:** Ray Kessler, ASE-certified mechanic with 22 years of experience
- **Open since:** 2009
- **Staff:** Ray, two mechanics (Dee and Marcus), and Ray's daughter Lena at the front desk
- **Tagline:** Honest repairs. Straight answers.
- **Cars they work on:** most domestic and Asian makes, cars and light trucks. No diesel, no European luxury brands.

### Policies and selling points

These are what set the shop apart; Ray wants them easy to spot.

- **Free written estimates.** No work starts until the customer approves the price.
- **Warranty:** 24 months or 24,000 miles on parts and labor.
- **Old parts returned** on request, so customers can see what was replaced.
- **Free shuttle** within 5 miles while the car is in the shop.
- **Appointments preferred** for repairs; walk-ins fine for oil changes and inspections.
- **Payment:** cash, all major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. No checks.

### Branding and photos

The shop has no logo, so the name set in a bold font is fine, or design a simple wordmark.

- **Colors:** dark navy and a safety orange accent, on white or light gray. Ray says no red, "it looks like a warning light."
- **Feel:** clean, solid and trustworthy. Not flashy, not a sports-car vibe.
- **Photos:** free stock photos from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) or [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com). Good searches: "mechanic working on car", "car on lift garage", "auto repair shop", "brake repair".
- Pick photos with no visible brand logos or recognizable faces up close, so they read as this shop.

### What Ray wants from the site

Ray will judge the site on his phone, since that's how most of his customers will see it. He hasn't named a budget; he'll say what it's worth to him once he sees it.

**Must have**

- Phone number visible right away on a phone, and tappable to call
- Hours and address easy to find, with a map or directions link
- Services and prices
- Works well on phones

**Nice to have**

- A simple appointment request form (name, phone, car, what's wrong)
- The reviews
- Some subtle polish; Ray's daughter said it should "feel modern"

**Deal-breakers for Ray**

- Hard to read: tiny text, low contrast, or busy backgrounds
- Animations that get in the way or make it feel slow
- Anything he'd have to log in to and maintain himself
